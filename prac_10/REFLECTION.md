# Reflection
Regarding the estimates that you did for practical tasks, how was your accuracy and did your accuracy improve during the course of the subject?

At the beginning of my study period, my ability to estimate the time required to complete tasks was not very precise. This was largely due to my initial unfamiliarity with the complexity of the coding tasks and the time each would require. As I continued with my studies and gained more hands-on experience, I developed a better understanding of how to approach and solve coding problems efficiently. My familiarity with the tools and the coding environment improved, which led to a more accurate assessment of how long various tasks would take. Over time, I noticed a significant improvement in my ability to estimate time requirements, reflecting a deeper understanding of the subject matter and better time management skills.

Regarding code reviews, what have you learned from both being reviewed and doing reviews?

Participating in code reviews, both as a reviewer and as someone being reviewed, has been a highly educational experience. From the perspective of conducting reviews, I've learned that achieving the same outcome in coding can be accomplished through a variety of methods. This has exposed me to different coding styles and techniques, enriching my understanding of problem-solving approaches and broadening my perspective on coding practices.

On the flip side, receiving feedback through reviews has highlighted areas where I need to improve. I realized that my program structure and coding logic could benefit from further refinement. Through the constructive criticism received, I became aware of my recurring mistakes and learned how to address them more effectively. This process of reviewing and being reviewed has been instrumental in enhancing my coding skills, helping me to recognize and implement best practices, and ultimately improving the quality of my code.

Regarding the practical tasks overall, what would you change if you were in charge of the subject?

If I had the opportunity to oversee the practical tasks of the subject, I would make a few adjustments to enhance the learning experience. One key area I would focus on is incorporating more exercises related to association and inheritance. These concepts are fundamental to object-oriented programming and are essential for developing a strong understanding of how different objects and classes interact with one another. By including additional exercises that delve deeper into these topics, students would gain a more comprehensive grasp of object-oriented principles, which would be beneficial for their overall programming skill set and for tackling more complex coding challenges in the future.
